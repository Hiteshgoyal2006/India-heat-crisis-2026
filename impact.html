AI & Data Science Impact

✓

Grid Stability: Predictive modeling anticipates cooling demand surges days ahead, preventing blackouts and keeping life-saving fans and ACs running at critical moments.

✓

Targeted Interventions: Granular mapping deploys water tankers and cooling centers to exact vulnerable streets before heatstroke cases spike — directly reducing mortality.

🏠

Simple Solutions Impact

✓

Immediate Cooling: Cool roofs reduce surface temperatures by 30°C and indoor temps by 2–5°C — enough to prevent heat exhaustion for those without air conditioning.

✓

Economic Savings: Reflective roofs cut electricity bills 30–40%, reducing strain on family budgets and the national power grid simultaneously.

✓

Survival for the Vulnerable: Wet gunny sacks on windows lower room temps 2–3°C — a small drop that often keeps elderly people and infants physiologically safe.

🔄

The Multiplier Effect

✓

Cooler Cities: 20–30% cool roofs with green cover can drop a city's ambient temperature 1–2°C — effectively cancelling the Urban Heat Island effect.

✓

Healthier Workforce: Workers suffer less dehydration and fatigue, protecting billions in economic output lost annually to heat-related productivity dips.

✓

Positive Feedback Loop: Cooler cities need less energy → fewer emissions → less future heat. Each solution reinforces all the others in a self-sustaining cycle.
