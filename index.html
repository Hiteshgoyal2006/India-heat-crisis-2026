Quick Overview

The Crisis at a Glance

Four facts that define the scale and urgency of India's unprecedented heatwave in April 2026.

🌡️

Record Temperatures

98 of the world's 100 hottest cities were Indian. Temperatures peaked between 44°C and 46°C across multiple states simultaneously.

🌆

Urban Heat Island

Concrete cities absorbed heat all day and radiated it at night, keeping temperatures above 30°C and trapping residents in a 24/7 "oven."

⚡

Grid Under Pressure

Record electricity demand for cooling pushed power grids to their limits, risking blackouts that could cut off life-saving fans and ACs.

😷

Health Emergency

Nighttime temperatures preventing natural cooling caused a sharp spike in heat-related illnesses, especially among the elderly and children.
