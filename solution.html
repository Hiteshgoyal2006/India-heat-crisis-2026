Our Response

Combating the Crisis

A two-pronged approach: cutting-edge AI & data science for large-scale resilience, combined with simple, accessible solutions anyone can act on today.

Two Approaches

Tech + Accessible Solutions

Neither approach alone is enough. High-tech interventions protect the infrastructure; grassroots solutions protect the people. Together they form a layered heat defence.

🤖

AI & Technology Solutions

Heat Vulnerability Mapping

Satellite imagery (Landsat 8) + ML maps urban heat islands to building level, pinpointing locations for life-saving cooling centers.

Predictive Energy Demand Modeling

XGBoost / LightGBM algorithms forecast electricity demand surges days ahead from real-time temperature data, preventing grid failures.

Smart Irrigation & Soil Monitoring

IoT soil moisture sensors automate watering in urban green belts, maximizing evapotranspiration to fight the "dry soil" feedback loop.

Tree Canopy AI

Google's Tree Canopy Tool identifies high heat-risk areas to prioritize tree planting where shade provides the greatest cooling benefit.

🌿

Simple Solutions for Everyone

Cool Roof Initiative

Solar-reflective roof coatings — even low-cost lime wash — can reduce indoor temperatures by up to 10°C, protecting millions without AC.

Personal Cooling Gear

Phase-change material (PCM) vests provide hours of core-body cooling for outdoor workers without any electricity requirement.

Community Blue-Green Spaces

Restoring local lakes and ponds as natural heat sinks. Restoring 6km of a Seoul stream lowered nearby temperatures by 6°C.

Strategic Greening & Off-Grid Cooling

Balcony plants cool air through transpiration. Solar fans maintain airflow during outages without adding to grid load.

The Strategy

A 4-Step Response Framework

Each solution targets a specific link in the heat chain. Applied together, they create a self-reinforcing positive loop — aligned with SDG 11 & SDG 13.

🗺️

Step 1 — Identify

AI mapping pinpoints exact streets and buildings most at risk so resources reach precisely where they are needed most.

🛡️

Step 2 — Protect

Cool roofs, PCM vests, and cooling centers provide immediate protection for the most vulnerable before heatstroke cases spike.

🌱

Step 3 — Cool

Tree planting, water body restoration, and smart irrigation lower the city's ambient temperature through natural processes.

🔋

Step 4 — Sustain

Predictive grid management prevents blackouts, keeping fans and ACs running — sustaining all other cooling efforts long-term.
